Privacy Policy — Tech Solutions Excel Assistant

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Your files stay on your computer

Excel and CSV files you open in Tech Solutions Excel Assistant are read and processed only inside the app on your own device. We do not upload, store, sell or share your files or their contents. We have no server that receives your data.

நீங்க திறக்கும் Excel / CSV ஃபைல்கள் உங்க கம்ப்யூட்டருக்குள்ளேயே மட்டும் process ஆகும். எந்த சர்வருக்கும் அனுப்பப்படாது, சேமிக்கப்படாது.

2. Microphone and voice commands

The microphone is used only when you press the microphone button to give a voice command. Speech is converted to text by your browser's built-in speech recognition service (for example, the one provided by Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome), which may process the audio on its own servers under that provider's privacy policy. The app itself does not record or keep any audio. Only the recognised text is used, on your device, to run the command. You can always type commands instead of speaking.

மைக் பட்டனை அழுத்தும் போது மட்டுமே மைக் பயன்படும். பேச்சை எழுத்தாக மாற்றுவது பிரவுசரின் speech சேவை. இந்த app ஆடியோவை பதிவு செய்யவோ சேமிக்கவோ செய்யாது.

3. No accounts, no tracking

The app has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no cookies used for tracking. The app files may be cached on your device so it can work offline.

4. Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
